Starting at the Recurse Center

This week, I started a six week batch at the Recurse Center in Downtown, Brooklyn. After the first week, I'm so impressed (and a bit overwhelmed) by my fellow batch mates and folks that have come through RC.

One thing that a lot of folks have in common is their ability to take succinct notes and their willingness to share those notes publically.

I've long neglected this site so I've made a few changes to the source code and enabled Atom feeds. I'm hoping this post will be the first of many as I progress through my batch and communicate my ideas and things I've learned.
